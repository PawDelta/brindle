1 · buttons

Three button schemes, each a full system

All three come from the website's tokens (pass green, ink, the hairlines, 4 px radius, Geist). Each one defines primary, secondary, quiet and destructive, with hover, pressed, focus and disabled, in both themes, and every text/ground pair is checked against WCAG AA. The scheme you pick here also dresses the app frame in section 2. Use 1–3 or the cards to switch. My pick is Neutral: it keeps green for what passed, as the site does, and the solid ink primary reads instantly in both themes. Solid is the runner-up for a louder main action.

States default · hover · pressed · focus · disabled · loading, both themes

In the app live: click any button. Nothing changes for 300 ms, then it dims and the indicator appears outside it.

At 200 ms nothing should show.
2 · milestones

Forty milestones in five rows

One goal, 40 milestones in 4 phases: 28 passed, 1 running, 1 failing, 10 upcoming. Every direction keeps the default view to about five rows however many milestones there are, puts colour only on what needs attention, and shows the same goal in both places: the sidebar's context pane and the session panel above the terminal. Everything is live: hover or scrub the segments and dots, open a phase, filter the full list. Use Q W E or the cards.

The frame's buttons wear the scheme chosen in section 1.
