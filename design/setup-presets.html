How much should your agents do on their own?

Agents stop and wait whenever they need your permission. Pick how often that happens. You can change this, and edit or remove any rule it adds, in Settings → Permissions.

◇With Autonomous, brindle also groups similar requests (for example every push into your default branch) so one answer covers them all.
Nothing is locked in. Every rule a preset adds can be edited or removed later.
