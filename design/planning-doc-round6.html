The gate"Start building" stays disabled, with the reason, until every required reviewer has signed off on the current revision. Optional reviewers never block it. On Pro, your own sign-off opens it.
Sign-offs and revisionsA new revision that changes a decision or the split resets sign-offs and re-notifies reviewers; wording-only edits keep them. You can turn that off per design.
Saved by defaultSharing a design saves it to the Library with every revision, comment and sign-off: the record of what the team agreed and why. You can turn it off when you share.
Changing reviewers laterAdding a required reviewer, or switching someone to Required, re-opens the gate unless they've already signed off on this revision. Switching to Optional or removing someone never loses what they wrote: comments, votes and sign-offs stay on the record.

Enterprise governance Enterprise plan

New. Org admins set review policies: a design that touches certain paths, carries a label, or any design at all must be approved by a team, a role or a named person before it's built. Members see those reviewers as locked rows they can't remove, the gate counts them, and a policy that isn't met blocks the build and says why.

Admin · Settings › Organization › Review policies

One row per policy: what it matches, then who it requires. It sits in the Organization section beside members, roles and approval limits.

Questions for you

Places where I had to pick something to draw it. Each is easy to change.

  1. Who can change reviewers after sharing? I drew it for the author. Should anyone with "can edit" be able to as well?
  2. After the build has started, should adding a required reviewer pause the workers, or only go on the record? I've assumed the list can still change but the build isn't stopped.
  3. A policy met by someone already on the list (Sam is a Staff engineer) adds no second sign-off. Is one sign-off counting twice what you want?
  4. No way around a policy. I haven't drawn an admin "waive once, with a reason". Do Enterprise teams need that escape hatch, or a stand-in for when a named person (Alex) is away?
  5. The Library's rail now leads with status and drops categories; the results panel is one line. Say if either should stay as it is today.
