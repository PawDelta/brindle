Required and optional reviewers, a build gate, and the Library

Round 4 stays as it was. This adds who must approve, the gate that holds the build until they all have, and where shared designs live: saved to the Library by default.

Sharing: choose required and optional reviewers

When you share, each person is required or optional. Required reviewers must sign off before anything is built. Optional reviewers can comment, vote and suggest, but don't hold the build. Saving to the Library is on by default for shared designs.

The gate"Start building" stays disabled, with the reason, until every required reviewer has signed off on the current revision. Optional reviewers never block it. On Pro, your own sign-off opens it.
Sign-offs and revisionsA new revision that changes a decision or the split resets sign-offs and re-notifies reviewers; wording-only edits keep them. You can turn that off per design.
Saved by defaultSharing a design saves it to the Library with every revision, comment and sign-off: the record of what the team agreed and why. You can turn it off when you share.
