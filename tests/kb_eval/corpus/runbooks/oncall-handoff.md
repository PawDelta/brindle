# On-call handoff

## Schedule

On-call rotates every Monday at 10:00 Pacific. The outgoing engineer writes
the handoff note; the incoming engineer acknowledges it in `#oncall`.

## What the note covers

Open incidents, silenced alerts with their expiry, and any deploy freezes in
effect. Alerts may be silenced for at most 48 hours.

## Escalation

If the primary does not acknowledge a page within 10 minutes, the secondary is
paged. After another 10 minutes the engineering manager is paged.
