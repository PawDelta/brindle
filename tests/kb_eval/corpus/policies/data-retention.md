# Data retention policy

## Operational logs

Application logs are kept for 30 days in hot storage, then archived for one
year and deleted.

## Customer shipment records

Shipment records are kept for 7 years to satisfy customs rules. Personal
contact details on a record are removed after 24 months.

## Deletion requests

A customer's deletion request must be completed within 30 days. The privacy
team tracks each request in the compliance tracker.
