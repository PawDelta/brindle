---
name: style/minimal-diff
description: The smallest diff that does the job
---
Make the smallest change that completes the task. Don't reformat, reorder or
rename what you aren't changing, don't fix unrelated problems you notice (name
them in your report instead), and don't add abstractions, options or comments
for cases the task doesn't have. Match the surrounding code's style exactly,
even where you would have written it differently. A reviewer should be able to
read the whole diff and see only the task.
